How the model works

A random forest learns from 3,000 simulated connections. The simulator assigns quality labels using upload speed, round-trip latency, jitter and packet loss, with random variation. Scores average the class proportions from the leaves of 41 decision trees.

This teaching model uses synthetic labels. Accuracy on real video calls is unverified. Enter your own measurements or explore a sample connection.
